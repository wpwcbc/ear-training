**Blocked by:** 03: Editable progressions and multi-note practice, 04: Guided Singing complete flow.

**Status:** ready-for-agent

- [ ] Stop, navigation, replacement playback and interruption cancel stale voices/callbacks and retain valid committed data. Offer recoverable playback without inventing grades.

- [ ] Browser verification covers both modes, fixed completion, Unlimited/Stop, historical replay after preset edits and source-record preservation; hardware listening remains a separate manual check.
