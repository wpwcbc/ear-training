# V2 Practice Surface

Mode: Operate. Reuse the existing forest/mint semantic tokens and local Lucide
brand asset without mounting the Legacy shell or loading its UI controllers.
Presets are repeated items; configuration, practice, results and Insights are
unframed task views. Native buttons, checkboxes, selects and details handle
interaction. Controls target 44px; inputs use 16px text. The degree grid has
stable tracks, and the labelled breakdown region scrolls horizontally on small
screens rather than compressing musical evidence. Keep objective choice scores
and self-assessment explicit. No marketing, decorative animation or framework.
