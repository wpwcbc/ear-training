Scale Degree Ear Training

Find the note fast. Name it faster.

Train instant recognition of scale degrees in context. Each question plays a chord in a low register, then a target upper note. Identify the degree, play it, name it, answer.

Quick guide

  1. Pick a Progression Preset or build your own in Chord Progression Setup.
  2. Set your preference in Controls. Press Start Audio, then Start Test.
  3. Play your test in Live Question. Use Replay if needed.
  4. Inspect the Records to analyze your progress!
