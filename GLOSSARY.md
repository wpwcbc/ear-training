# Jazz Ear Lab

Language for harmonic hearing practice, as defined by the confirmed project
intent in jazz-ear-lab.md and the V2 Release 1 specifications.

## Language

**Question**:
A single harmonic hearing task with a known chord and target degree, answered
through self-assessed audiation in M1 or objectively scored recognition in M2.
_Avoid_: Session (a question is not a whole practice session).

**Question lifecycle**:
The course of one practice question, from presentation through listening,
response and reference review until it is left or practice ends. An interruption
and recovery can occur within the same question without replacing its first
response evidence.
_Avoid_: Session lifecycle (which also includes starting and ending practice).

**Chord quality**:
The chord type independent of its root, such as maj7, m7 or 7alt. Cmaj7 and
Fmaj7 share a chord quality but are different chords.
_Avoid_: Chord (when only its quality is meant).

**Per-quality target pool**:
The selected degrees that may become practice targets for one chord quality,
including any selected chord tones, extensions and alterations.
_Avoid_: Extension pool (the selection is not limited to extensions).

**Answer options**:
The degree choices available when answering a recognition question, distinct
from the pool of sounds eligible to become its target.
_Avoid_: Target pool (when referring to response choices).

**Chord-balanced sampling**:
A question distribution giving selected chord qualities equal representation,
with their eligible target degrees balanced within each quality.
_Avoid_: Pair-balanced sampling (which instead balances every quality/degree pair).

**Voicing voice**:
A note's original low-to-high position in the complete chord voicing, before
any target exclusion. This identity is distinct from its chord-relative degree.
_Avoid_: Remaining-note position (which can change when a target is omitted).

**Voicing mix**:
The relative levels of the original chord-voicing voices, distinct from overall
volume and from the independently sounded target.
_Avoid_: Target volume (voicing mix controls accompaniment, not the target voice).

**Audition**:
Listening to the mixed full chord accompaniment in Customize, without an
independent target voice or a recorded practice attempt.
_Avoid_: Practice question (an audition is not assessed practice or target exposure).

**Overall Insights**:
The combined V2 results view organized by shared information categories, with
the contributing modules available as detail.
_Avoid_: Module comparison only (Overall includes actual cross-module totals).

**Production category**:
The Insights grouping of a production task into Imagined / sung or Instrumental
playing; a recognition-only module has no required production category.
_Avoid_: Stored response method (imagined and sung remain distinct raw values).

**Combined degree accuracy**:
The total first-correct results divided by total assessed questions from the
contributing V2 modules, accepting self-ratings and selection grades equally.
_Avoid_: Average module accuracy (module percentages are not averaged).

**Progression question**:
A harmonic-hearing task spanning a known sequence of chords and its ordered
melodic targets, heard or performed as a complete progression.
_Avoid_: Session (a session may contain multiple progression questions).

**Target position**:
One ordered melodic-note position within a particular chord occurrence of a
progression question; its degree is relative to that occurrence's chord.
_Avoid_: Voicing voice (a target position belongs to the melody, not accompaniment).

**Progression answer board**:
The single response surface containing all target positions of a progression
question, allowing answers to be filled in any order before joint submission.
_Avoid_: Per-note question screen.

**Progression note outcome**:
The result for one target position: a recognition selection grade or a guided
singing self-rating, distinct from a whole-progression summary.
_Avoid_: Whole-run rating (one overall rating cannot establish separate note outcomes).
