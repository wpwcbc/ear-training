# Jazz Ear Lab

Language for harmonic hearing practice, as defined by the confirmed project
intent in jazz-ear-lab.md and the V2 Release 1 specifications.

## Language

**Question**:
A single harmonic hearing task with a known chord and target degree, answered
through self-assessed audiation in M1 or objectively scored recognition in M2.
_Avoid_: Session (a question is not a whole practice session).

**Question lifecycle**:
The course of one practice question, from presentation through listening,
response and reference review until it is left or practice ends. An interruption
and recovery can occur within the same question without replacing its first
response evidence.
_Avoid_: Session lifecycle (which also includes starting and ending practice).
